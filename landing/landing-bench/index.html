02 — ARCHITECTURAL TOPOLOGY

Isolation is truth.

Instead of forcing a single non-Euclidean geometry to handle both distance and arrays, Helmlab isolates them. Two mathematical topologies mapped to the identical origin source, ensuring the proper tool for the exact mathematical objective to avoid matrix singularities.

MS
OBJ: ΔE MEASUREMENT

MetricSpace (v21)

Applies local volumetric distortion to align perfectly with human visual ellipses (MacAdam). Exclusively purposed for finding the exact perceptual distance between two colors. Validated against multi-corpus empirical data.

AXIS J', a', b'
BEYOND Do not use for arrays.

OBJ: INTERPOLATION

GenSpace (v1)

Keeps global topology entirely flat across opponent planes. Mathematically ensures linear transitions — preventing hue spiraling and chroma clipping when generating gradients or multi-stop palettes.

AXIS Lg, Cg, Hg
BEYOND Requires float64 precision.

05 — LOGGED CONSTRAINTS

Scientific Integrity

Perceptual uniformity in a non-Euclidean 3D space cannot be mapped mathematically without encountering some degree of singularity. For full transparency, below are three proven scenarios where our models hit boundaries or lose to legacy constraints.

§ MILD OVERFITTING

Under MetricSpace v21, we track a +1.8 STRESS gap between the full-data train block and the held-out 20% test block (Train: 22.14 → Test: 23.91). While remaining functionally superior, isolated generalizability loss is verified.

§ LEGACY DOMINANCE

CIEDE2000 still numerically outperforms MetricSpace on 3 of the 6 COMBVD micro-data sets (specifically LEEDS, RIT-DuPont, and WITT). MetricSpace achieves its ultimate aggregate dominance via overwhelming victory on the massive BFD-P set.

§ FLOAT PRECISION

GenSpace sRGB 16.7M full-cycle roundtrip variance is logged at 5.64e-08. While thoroughly sub-visual (human threshold sits roughly at ~1e-4), strictly mathematically, OKLab holds a purer float threshold (1.67e-15).
